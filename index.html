🔐 PRIVACY BOUNDARY

This prototype is local-first. It uses browser-observable signals and stores its history locally. It does not read SMS, calls, installed apps, private files, passwords, IMEI, or data belonging to other apps. A changed or novel result is not proof of hacking, malware, or compromise.
